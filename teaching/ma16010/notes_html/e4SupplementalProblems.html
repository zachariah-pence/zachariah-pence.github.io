Problem 1.

The error function is defined as:

\[ \operatorname{erf}(x) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} \int_0^x e^{-t^2} \, dt \]

  1. Show that \( \int_a^b e^{-t^2} \, dt = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}[\operatorname{erf}(b) - \operatorname{erf}(a)] \). [Hint: Combine the right-hand-side into 1 integral].

    Follow the hint:

    \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\left[\operatorname{erf}(b) - \operatorname{erf}(a)\right] = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\left[ \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} \int_0^b e^{-t^2} \, dt - \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} \int_0^a e^{-t^2} \, dt \right] \\[0.5em] = \int_0^b e^{-t^2} \, dt - \int_0^a e^{-t^2} \, dt = \int_0^b e^{-t^2} \, dt + \int_a^0 e^{-t^2} \, dt \\[0.5em] = \int_a^b e^{-t^2} \, dt \end{gathered} \]

  2. Approximate the value of erf(2) by using 6 trapezoids. Round to 4 decimal places.

    \[ \operatorname{erf}(2) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} \int_0^2 e^{-t^2} \, dt \approx \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} T_6 . \qquad \text{Find } \Delta x = \frac{2-0}{6} = \frac{1}{3} \] \[ \begin{aligned} T_6 &= \frac{1/3}{2}\left[ f(0) + 2f\!\left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right) + 2f\!\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right) + 2f(1) + 2f\!\left(\tfrac{4}{3}\right) + 2f\!\left(\tfrac{5}{3}\right) + f(2) \right] \\ &= \frac{1}{6}\left[ 1 + 2e^{-\frac{1}{9}} + 2e^{-\frac{4}{9}} + 2e^{-1} + 2e^{-\frac{16}{9}} + 2e^{-\frac{25}{9}} + e^{-4} \right] \\ &= \frac{1}{6}\left[ 1 + 1.7897 + 1.2824 + 0.7358 + 0.3380 + 0.1244 + 0.0183 \right] \\ &= \frac{1}{6}\left[ 5.2886 \right] = 0.8814 \end{aligned} \]

    So, \( \operatorname{erf}(2) \approx \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}} T_6 = \frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}(0.8814) = 0.9946 \)

    For context, \( \operatorname{erf}(2) \approx 0.9953 22 \ldots \)

Problem 2.

Compute the following integral:

\[ \begin{gathered} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin^2\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) \, dx \\[0.5em] \left[ \text{Hint: } \sin^2\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) = \frac{1-\cos(x)}{2} \right] \\[0.5em] \int \sin^2\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) dx \overset{\text{Hint}}{=\!=} \int \left( \frac{1-\cos x}{2} \right) dx \\[0.5em] = \frac{1}{2} \int (1 - \cos x) \, dx \\[0.5em] = \frac{1}{2}\left[ x - \sin x \right] + C \end{gathered} \]

So,

\[ \begin{gathered} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin^2\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) dx = \frac{1}{2}\left[ x - \sin x \right]_{-\pi}^{\pi} \\[0.5em] = \frac{1}{2}\left[ (\pi - 0) - (-\pi - 0) \right] \\[0.5em] = \frac{1}{2}\left[ 2\pi - 0 + 0 \right] \\[0.5em] = \frac{1}{2}\left[ 2\pi \right] \\[0.5em] = \pi \end{gathered} \]

Problem 3.

The marginal cost of manufacturing \( x \) yards of a certain fabric is \( C'(x) = 3 - 0.01x + 0.000006x^2 \) (in dollars per yard). Find the increase in cost if the production level is raised from 2000 yards to 4000 yards.

Need to find \( C(4000) - C(2000) \)

\[ \begin{gathered} C(4000) - C(2000) \;\overset{\substack{\text{FTC}/ \\ \text{Net Change Thm}}}{=\!=\!=}\; \int_{2000}^{4000} C'(t)\, dt \\[0.5em] \int_{2000}^{4000} C'(t)\, dt = \int_{2000}^{4000} \left[ 3 - 0.01x + 0.000006x^2 \right] dx \\[0.5em] = \left[ 3x - 0.01 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} x^2 + 0.000006 \cdot \tfrac{1}{3} x^3 \right]_{2000}^{4000} \\[0.5em] = \left[ 3(4000) - \frac{0.01}{2}(4000)^2 + \frac{0.000006}{3}(4000)^3 \right] - \left[ 3(2000) - \frac{0.01}{2}(2000)^2 + \frac{0.000006}{3}(2000)^3 \right] \\[0.5em] = 60000 - 2000 = \$58000 \end{gathered} \]

Problem 4.

The half-life of Radium-226 (\( {}^{226}_{88}\mathrm{Ra} \)) is 1590 years.

  1. If a sample of \( {}^{226}_{88}\mathrm{Ra} \) has a mass of 100mg, what percentage of the sample is left after 1000 years?

    Determine \( k \):

    \[ \begin{gathered} \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 100 = 100 e^{1590k} \\[0.5em] \tfrac{1}{2} = e^{1590k} \\[0.5em] \ln \tfrac{1}{2} = 1590k \\[0.5em] k = \frac{\ln \tfrac{1}{2}}{1590} = -\frac{\ln 2}{1590} \approx 0.000436 \end{gathered} \]

    So, \( P_{(t)} = 100 e^{-0.000436t} \)

    Compute \( P_{(1000)} = 100 e^{-0.000436(1000)} = 100 e^{-0.436} \approx 65\text{mg} \)

    \( \left[ \frac{65}{100} \right] \cdot 100 = 65\% \) of the sample is left

  2. When will the mass be reduced to 30mg?

    \[ \begin{gathered} 30 = 100 e^{kt} \\[0.5em] \tfrac{3}{10} = e^{kt} \\[0.5em] \ln \tfrac{3}{10} = kt \\[0.5em] t = -\frac{\ln(3/10)}{k} \end{gathered} \]

    We found \( k = -\frac{\ln 2}{1590} \), so

    \[ t = -\frac{\ln(3/10)}{\left(-\frac{\ln 2}{1590}\right)} = \left(\frac{\ln\left(\frac{3}{10}\right)}{\ln 2}\right)(1590) \approx 2761.78 \]

    It takes roughly \( 2761.78 \) years to reduce the sample to 30 mg.

Problem 5.

How long will be take for an investment to double if the interest rate is 6% compounded continuously?

\[ \begin{gathered} 2 = 1 e^{0.06t} \\[0.5em] \ln(2) = 0.06t \\[0.5em] t = \frac{\ln(2)}{0.06} \\[0.5em] t \approx 11.55 \text{ years} \end{gathered} \]
