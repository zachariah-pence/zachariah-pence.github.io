Lecture 29: Definite Integrals (Computing Areas)

Goal: Interpret the definite integral as the (signed) area underneath the graph of a function.

Recall Left/Right Riemann sums (\( L_N \) and \( R_N \)) are used to approximate the (signed) area underneath the graph of a function.

Question: What happens when we take \( N \to \infty \)? See what happens with a Desmos presentation (link here).

\[ \lim_{N \to \infty} L_N = \lim_{N \to \infty} R_n = \text{The exact area} \]

Definition. The (definite) integral of a function \( f \) from \( a \) to \( b \) is denoted as follows:

\[ \begin{gathered} \int_a^b f(x)\ dx \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \lim_{N \to \infty} L_N = \lim_{N \to \infty} R_n \\[0.5em] = \text{The signed area between the graph of } f \text{ and the } x\text{-axis on } [a,b]. \end{gathered} \]

\( a \) and \( b \) are called the upper and lower limits (or bounds) of integration, respectively. If the integral is finite, we say \( f \) is (Riemann) integrable on \( [a,b] \).

Example 1. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{-1}^{2} 3\ dx \)

\[ \begin{gathered} \int_{-1}^{2} 3\, dx = [\text{Area of Rectangle}] \\[0.5em] = 3\,(2 - (-1)) \\[0.5em] = 3(3) \\[0.5em] = 9 \end{gathered} \]

NOTE:

In general, if \( k \) is a constant:

\[ \int_a^b k\ dx = k\,(b-a) \]

Example 2. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{1}^{5} (2 - |x - 3|)\ dx \)

\[ \begin{gathered} \int_1^5 (2 - |x-3|)\,dx = [\text{Area of Triangle}] \\[0.5em] = \frac{1}{2}(4)(2) \\[0.5em] = 4 \end{gathered} \]

Example 3. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{0}^{3} (x - 1)\ dx \)

\[ \int_0^3 (x-1)\,dx = \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of the}\\ \text{Blue Triangle}\end{array}\right] - \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of the}\\ \text{Red Triangle}\end{array}\right] \] \[ \begin{gathered} = \frac{1}{2}(2)(2) - \frac{1}{2}(1)(1) \\[0.5em] = 2 - \frac{1}{2} \\[0.5em] = \frac{3}{2} \end{gathered} \]

Example 4. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{0}^{4} \left(\frac{1}{2}x + 1\right)\ dx \)

  1. METHOD 1:

    NOTE:

    The area of a trapezoid is \( A = \frac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h \)

    \[ \begin{gathered} \int_0^4 \left(\tfrac{1}{2}x + 1\right)dx = [\text{Area of the Trapezoid}] \\[0.5em] = \frac{1}{2}(3+1)(4) \\[0.5em] = 8 \end{gathered} \]
  1. METHOD 2: \[ \int_0^4 \left(\tfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)dx = \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of}\\ \text{Yellow Triangle}\end{array}\right] - \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of}\\ \text{Blue Triangle}\end{array}\right] \] \[ \begin{gathered} = \frac{1}{2}(6)(3) - \frac{1}{2}(2)(1) \\[0.5em] = 9 - 1 \\[0.5em] = 8 \end{gathered} \]

NOTE (Additivity of Integrals Example):

Rewriting METHOD 2 using integrals, we get:

\[ \int_0^4 \left(\frac{1}{2}x+1\right)dx = \int_{-2}^{4}\left(\tfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)dx - \int_{-2}^{0}\left(\tfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)dx \]

I.e.,

\[ \int_{-2}^{4}\left(\frac{1}{2}x+1\right)dx = \int_{-2}^{0}\left(\tfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)dx + \int_{0}^{4}\left(\tfrac{1}{2}x+1\right)dx \]

Example 5. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{1-x^2}\ dx \)

\[ \begin{gathered}\int_0^1 \sqrt{1-x^2}\,dx = \frac{1}{4}\left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of Circle}\\ \text{with radius }1\end{array}\right] \\ = \frac{1}{4}\left(\pi \cdot 1^2\right) \\[0.5em] = \frac{\pi}{4} \end{gathered} \]

Example 6. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} \sqrt{1-\frac{x^2}{2^2}}\ dx \)

NOTE:

The area of an ellipse that is described by the equation

\[ \frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1 \]

is \( \pi ab \).

\[ \int_{-2}^{2}\sqrt{1-\frac{x^2}{2^2}}\,dx = \frac{1}{2}\left[\text{Area of the ellipse}\right] = \frac{1}{2}\pi(2)(1) = \pi \]

Example 7. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{1}^{1} x^2\ dx \)

\[ \int_1^1 x^2\,dx = \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of the}\\ \text{Line Segment}\\\end{array}\right] =0\]

NOTE:

In general,

\[ \int_a^a f(x)~dx = 0\]

Example 8. Compute \( \displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin x\ dx \)

The area of the positive region is the same as the negative region. So,

\[ \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin x\,dx = 0 \]

Example 9. Consider the shaded region below:

  1. Describe the area of the shaded region using an integral:

    \[ \int_0^{10} |x-5|\,dx \]

  2. Compute the area of the shaded region.

\[ \int_0^{10} |x-5|\,dx = \frac{1}{2}(5)(5) + \frac{1}{2}(5)(5) = 25 \]

Example 10. Consider the shaded region below:

  1. Describe the area of the shaded region using an integral:

    \[ \int_{-4}^{3} f(x)\,dx \text{ where } f(x) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } x > 0 \\ -1 & \text{if } x < 0 \end{cases} \]

  2. Compute the area of the shaded region.

\[ \int_{-4}^{3} f(x)\,dx = 3(1) - 4(1) = -1 \]

Example 11. Consider the shaded region below:

  1. Describe the area of the shaded region using an integral:

    \[ \int_{-3}^{3}\left(1 + \sqrt{3^2 - x^2}\right)dx = \int_{-3}^{3}\left(1 + \sqrt{9 - x^2}\right)dx \]

  1. Compute the area of the shaded region.

\[ \int_{-3}^{3}\left(1+\sqrt{9-x^2}\right)dx = \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of the}\\ \text{Rectangle}\end{array}\right] + \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{Area of the}\\ \text{Semicircle}\end{array}\right] = 6(1) + \frac{1}{2}\pi(3)^2 = 6 + \frac{9}{2}\pi \]

NOTE (Linearity of Integrals Example):

Rewriting our work using integrals, we see:

\[ \int_{-3}^{3}(1+\sqrt{9-x^2})\,dx = \int_{-3}^{3} 1\,dx + \int_{-3}^{3}\sqrt{9-x^2}\,dx \]
